# Lecture 16: How to Build an Invoice and Expense Logger Using the GitHub Copilot Harness, Excel and a Teams Approval for Morgan

**In this folder:** Invoice-and-Expense-Logger-Build-Sheet.docx

**Resources:** Pashun Sports Invoice and Expense Log.xlsx, Pashun Sports Expense Policy.docx, Ridgeline Apparel Supply Invoice RAS-2291.docx, Swift Courier Co Receipt SC-88317.docx, Bright Ink Print Shop Invoice BI-4421.docx *(in the Resources folder)*

Build the agent with Invoice-and-Expense-Logger-Build-Sheet.docx, swapping in your own company, supplier, approver and threshold, and use a copy of Pashun Sports Invoice and Expense Log.xlsx as the starting layout for your own ExpenseLog workbook. Add Pashun Sports Expense Policy.docx and Ridgeline Apparel Supply Agreement.docx as knowledge (or your own expense policy and supplier agreement). For the demo tests, attach Swift Courier Co Receipt SC-88317.docx and Ridgeline Apparel Supply Invoice RAS-2291.docx (save it as a PDF first to match the demo). Bright Ink Print Shop Invoice BI-4421.docx is optional, for trying a returning-user test yourself in Your Turn.

## To follow along exactly

In Excel on your own computer, open Pashun Sports Invoice and Expense Log.xlsx, select the data, choose Format as Table and name the table ExpenseLog.

On your own SharePoint site, upload the workbook to a Finance document library and publish Pashun Sports Expense Policy.docx as a page or file there.
