# Lecture 14: How to Build a Business Travel Helper That Shortlists Hotels for Approval Using Computer Use

**In this folder:** Business-Travel-Helper-Build-Sheet.docx

**Resources:** Pashun Sports Travel Policy.docx *(in the Resources folder)*

Build the agent with Business-Travel-Helper-Build-Sheet.docx, swapping in your own manager, nightly cap and cities. Add Pashun Sports Travel Policy.docx as Knowledge (or your own five-rule travel policy), then test the Search Hotels tool on its own before testing the whole agent. For the demo test, use Manchester, 12 December 2026 to 14 December 2026 and a $150 nightly cap, and give your own approver's email address when the agent asks.

## To follow along exactly

Computer use is a preview feature. Check that it is available in your region and that your Power Platform environment has capacity for it before you start.

Upload Pashun Sports Travel Policy.docx to your own SharePoint site or keep it on your computer, so you can add it to the agent as Knowledge.
