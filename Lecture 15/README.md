## To follow along exactly

Pay-as-you-go billing must be attached to your environment before you build, because goal-driven agents on the GitHub Copilot harness use Copilot Credits from the first build action. Copilot-Studio-Credits-Setup-Steps.pdf shows the steps.

To follow the demo exactly, create a Planner plan called Pashun Pro Tee US Launch from the launch plan file, connect the Excel workbook with its USForecast and SalesSummary tables, and put the three Ridgeline emails in your inbox.
